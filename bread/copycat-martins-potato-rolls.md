# Copycat Martin's Potato Rolls

**Yield:** 8 hamburger buns or 16 hot dog buns

## Ingredients

- 200 g flour
- 75 g potato flour
- tangzhong: 25 g flour + 100 g water
- 25 g sugar
- 7 g salt
- 7 g instant yeast
- 20 g dry milk powder
- 14 g melted butter
- 20 g veg oil
- 100 g water
- 1/8 tsp turmeric
- .6 g sodium propionate (try 1/16 tsp)
- 1.5 tsp dough conditioner
- some gluten?
