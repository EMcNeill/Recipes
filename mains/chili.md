# Chili

You knoooooow it!

## Method

**Sear and set aside:**

- 1/2 Tbsp oil
- 1/2 lb beef chuck, seasoned and cubed
- 1 lb 93% fat free ground turkey, seasoned

**Cook until fragrant:**

- 1/2 Tbsp oil
- 1 jalapeno, seeded and chopped
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/8 tsp MSG- freshly ground pepper
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano

**Blend:**

- 1 chipotle canned in adobo sauce
- 1 Tbsp adobo sauce
- 3 dried New Mexico chiles, seeded chopped and microwaved til toasty (~10+10 seconds)
- 3 cups chicken broth

**Combine all and add:**

- 1 can pinto beans

**Simmer for 3 hours, then finish off with:**

- 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 1.5 Tbsp corn meal
- Salt to taste (maybe 1/4 tsp more)
