# Beef & Barley Soup

**Yield:** 1450 Calories per batch

## Ingredients

- 1 pound lean ground beef
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 14.5 oz. can diced tomatoes
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- Red pepper flakes [optional]
- 6 cups beef broth
- 1/2 cup pearl barley
- 2 cups Carrots, chopped
- Salt and pepper (~1.5 T. of each)

## Method

1. Chop the onions and garlic. In a large pot, cook the ground beef over medium-high heat, breaking it up with a wooden spoon as it starts to brown. Stir in the onion and garlic, lower the heat to medium and cook, stirring occasionally, until the beef is well browned, 12 to 15 minutes.
2. Stir in the tomatoes, oregano, and red pepper flakes [if using], and bring to a simmer over high heat. Stir in the broth, barley, carrots, and celery and return to a simmer. Lower the heat and cook until the barley is very tender, about 1 hour.
3. Stir in the parsley; season with salt and pepper.
