# Banana Bran Bread

Whole wheat banana bread with added wheat bran. About 53g fiber per loaf (~5g per slice at 10 slices).

## Ingredients

- 8 tbsp (113g) unsalted butter, melted
- 2/3 cup (142g) dark brown sugar
- 1/4 cup (85g) honey
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups (340g) mashed banana (~3 medium)
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 3–4 tbsp milk or buttermilk
- 50g (~3/4 cup) wheat bran
- 1 3/4 cups (215g) whole wheat flour
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp table salt

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 325°F. Grease a 9x5 loaf pan.
2. Blend butter, brown sugar, honey, eggs, banana, vanilla, and milk until smooth.
3. Blend in the wheat bran. Let the mixture sit 15–20 minutes so the bran hydrates. The batter should be thick but pourable; add another tablespoon of milk if it's stiff.
4. Whisk together flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, baking soda, baking powder, and salt.
5. Fold the dry ingredients into the wet mix by hand until just combined. Don't blend — it overworks the gluten.
6. Pour into the pan and bake 60 minutes, then 25 more minutes tented with foil.
7. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack.

## Notes

- Leavening goes in with the flour, not during the bran rest — baking soda starts reacting with the banana and brown sugar immediately.
- The original recipe used 270g flour and no bran. Swapping 55g flour for 50g bran adds ~15g fiber net.
- For more bran (60–70g): drop flour to ~190g, add 2 tbsp more milk and 1 tbsp melted butter. Past ~25% of flour weight it stops tasting like banana bread.
