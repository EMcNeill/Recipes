# E's Salsa

**Yield:** Yields about 6 cups  
**Source:** <https://docs.google.com/document/d/14sXsS7ZRzzokDRAUs087VnWip3cWvTWIREq6QXt1cXs/edit>

## Ingredients

- 2 lb tomatoes (grape, cherry, roma all work well)
- 1 large chopped onion
- 1 can tomato paste
- 1/3 cup white vinegar
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2-4 to-go cups of Chipotle’s green medium salsa

## Method

1. Instructions: Blend all ingredients. Bring to a boil, then simmer until desired it reaches your preferred thickness, usually around 45 minutes. Chill overnight before serving.
